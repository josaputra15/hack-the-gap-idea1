Accessibility

Last updated: October 17, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Pinwire. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we do

  • List view: every story on the map is also available in a simple list (the "List" button, or the "Skip to the news list" link).
  • Keyboard: all flags, story circles, and buttons work with Tab, Enter, and Esc. In stories, use ← and → to move and Space to pause.
  • Screen readers: buttons have clear labels, and updates (votes, new comments) are announced.
  • Motion: the app respects your device's "reduce motion" setting. Stories never move on by themselves when it is on, and you can always pause them.
  • Contrast: text and controls meet AA contrast in both dark and light themes.
  • Plain English: headlines use simple words and short sentences.
  • Zoom: the layout works at 200% zoom and on small screens.

Known limits

The map itself is visual. Use the list view or search for the same content without the map.
